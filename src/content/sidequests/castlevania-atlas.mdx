Netflix's *Castlevania* and *Castlevania: Nocturne* are set in real places, fifteenth-century Wallachia and revolutionary France, and name real towns: Târgoviște, Brăila, Genoa, Paris. But they also invent places, and give them away in dialogue: Gresit is "close to Arges," the Magician's town is "thirty miles up the road" from Genoa.

So I set out to put it all on one map. The question is where the invented places go, and the honest answer is that you don't always know.

## Drawing the uncertainty

Most fan maps put a pin on everything and look equally confident about all of it. I graded each of the 27 places by how much is actually known:

- **Real place**: a real town at its real coordinates.
- **Placed from clues**: invented, positioned from what characters say,
  and drawn with a dashed ring showing how wrong it might be.
- **Region only**: the show names a region, not a place.
- **Location unrevealed**: drawn in a labelled box rather than guessed.

The dashed rings are the part worth the effort. A pin says *here*. A
ring says *somewhere in this area, and here's how big the area is* —
which is the truthful thing to say about a town described as thirty
miles from somewhere else.
